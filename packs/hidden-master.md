# Pack: hidden-master 战神扮猪

## Legal

- `Mask` stays below `True` for a long time. Early public \(P\) is illegal.
- `true_pay` hits `Face_opponent` and `P_lead` via a **visible** token or skill
  residue (dog tag, old wound, a salute from a third).
- Street brawls of 5+ bodies are visually illegal; downgrade to two-shot + sound.
