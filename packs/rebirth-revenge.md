# Pack: rebirth-revenge 重生复仇

Legal table, not a plot template.

## Legal

- One time reset allowed. Second reset forbidden. Past facts enter the secret
  library tagged `already_happened`.
- Lead `Mask` may stay below `True` until identity tickets.
- `true_pay` must move `Face_opponent` and/or `identity`. A slap without a
  visible token is illegal.
- Opening crime must have a token that can appear in ep 1.

## Defaults

- Basket: 打脸 0.45 / 甜 0.05 / 虐 0.15 / 燃 0.15 / 智斗 0.05 / 身份 0.15
- `power_slope`: stairs
- `first_gate`: 8 · `free_window`: 8 · `ep_seconds`: 90
- Motifs: one token, one reversed line, one staircase or banquet
