# Pack: sweet-ceo 霸总甜宠

## Legal

- `Desire` rise may replace part of `Face` damage.
- `public_punish` down-weighted; if used, it hits the blocker not the lover.
- `true_pay` is a public stand / claim, not a corpse-reveal.

## Defaults

- Basket: 打脸 0.15 / 甜 0.40 / 虐 0.15 / 燃 0.05 / 智斗 0.05 / 身份 0.20
- `power_slope`: growth on the lead's Face, steep on the CEO's Desire
- Motifs: one nickname, one coat, one private kitchen / car
