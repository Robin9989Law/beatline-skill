# On camera — 这不是小说

A beat that only lives in a character's head is not a beat. Vertical short
drama is bodies, tokens, and looks inside a 9:16 frame, 3–8 seconds.

**If the audience cannot see it on a person, it does not exist.**

## The test

Read `must_show` out loud. Ask:

1. Who is in frame? (1–2 people from `cast_on_screen`)
2. What body part or token moves?
3. What does the other person do as reaction, or is this a single insert?
4. Can this be shot without a narrator?

If any answer is "they realize / they remember / the reader knows", **fail**.
Rewrite as a visible verb: turn the bracelet, drop the paper, not sit down,
salute that does not complete, open the car door on the wrong person.

## Shootable verbs (legal)

拍桌、递文件、耳光、推门、转信物、松手、整理头发、挡人、开后排、
袖口滑开、把协议拍在桌上、敬礼未落下、把大衣搭上肩、把刮痕转到灯下。

Downgrade anything else:

| Novel move | On-camera replacement |
|---|---|
| 她意识到这是谋杀 | 手停在刮痕上，对方的笑还没收 |
| 他想起三年前 | 闪回只给手/物，一拍，然后切回现在的反应 |
| 全场哗然 | 一杯酒停住；第三人只给反应特写 |
| 心中暗发誓 | 把刀/镐/牌按回去，眼神不解释 |
| 与此同时庄园里 | 禁止。换场要单独票，且花预算 |
| 三天后 | 禁止。用信物/伤口/服装连续，不要时间字幕 |

## Banned on tickets and in instance

`心想 内心 意识到 感到 想到 回忆起 其实 原来 旁白 心中 暗暗
他不知道 她不知道 观众知道 与此同时 三天后 这一集讲了`

Emotion is a face, a hand, a withheld line — never a sentence about feeling.

## Visual layer still binds

- 竖屏对峒 1–2 人。第三人 = 下一票的反应特写，不是群像。
- 复杂群殴、细手指法、交叉走位：降级动作，不降级功能。
- 五人宴会打脸 → 门外二人，宴会只给声音。
